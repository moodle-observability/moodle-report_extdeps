# Changelog

## 0.1.2 - 2026-11-25

- Credits of the Moodle Observability family; first public release.

## 0.1.1 - 2026-11-24

- The pages use the available width (up to 1400 px), also in themes whose report layout shrinks to the content.
- Fewer columns; what to do is shown once per status, in the cards of the course.
- Only the broken dependencies by default, with a link to show all of them; short dates.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-24

- Site view with cards per provider and the list of courses, all occurrences, course view with what to do, Edit,
  History and Check now, dependency page with the history and false positives, on demand analysis with its result
  page, and the "External dependencies" source of custom reports.
